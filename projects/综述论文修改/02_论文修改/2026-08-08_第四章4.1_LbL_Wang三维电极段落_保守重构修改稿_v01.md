## 英文修改稿

Building on these foundations, Wang et al. [42] extended LbL assembly to a complex three-dimensional electrode configuration. They dispersed different lithium-intercalation active particles in negatively charged carboxymethyl cellulose (CMC). Electrostatic repulsion helped limit nanoparticle aggregation in the dispersion. A non-conductive, porous three-dimensional melamine resin foam (MRF) served as the insulating substrate. Active thin films were assembled on the internal surfaces of its microchannels. High-molecular-weight polyethyleneimine (PEI) and tetrakis(4-aminophenyl)porphyrin (TAPA) were introduced to adjust the spacing between conductive and intercalation phases (Figure 9b). The resulting architecture arranged active materials and conductive agents along the tortuous pores of the 3D sponge. This configuration differs from the blind, random percolation pathways described for conventional slurry coating. The 3D arrangement may help reduce anisotropic stress concentration and shorten local transport paths for ions and electrons.

## 中文对照

在此基础上，Wang 等[42]将 LbL 组装拓展至复杂的三维电极构型。该研究将不同的锂离子嵌入活性颗粒分散于带负电的羧甲基纤维素（CMC）中。静电排斥有助于限制分散体系中的纳米颗粒团聚。研究选用非导电的多孔三维三聚氰胺树脂泡沫（MRF）作为绝缘基底。活性薄膜随后组装在其内部微通道表面。研究进一步引入高分子量聚乙烯亚胺（PEI）和小分子四（4-氨基苯基）卟啉（TAPA），以调节导电相与嵌入相之间的间距（Figure 9b）。所得结构使活性材料和导电组分沿三维海绵的曲折孔道分布。该构型不同于传统浆料涂布中所描述的盲端、随机贯通路径。三维排布可能有助于降低各向异性应力集中，并缩短离子和电子的局部传输路径。
