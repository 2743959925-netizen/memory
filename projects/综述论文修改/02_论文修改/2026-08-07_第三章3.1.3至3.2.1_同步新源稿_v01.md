## 3.1.3 Dynamic Covalent Bonds

Dynamic covalent chemistry introduces exchangeable covalent bonds into assemblies and polymer networks [53]. These bonds can retain covalent connectivity under one set of conditions and exchange under another, allowing network rearrangement without complete dissolution. In self-assembly, this reversibility supports error correction and reorganization, while non-covalent interactions continue to determine how the resulting species associate. Wang et al. used dynamic covalent bond formation to construct azobenzene–galactopyranoside supra-amphiphiles with dual-responsive assembly behavior [52]. The assembled structure therefore arose from both covalent exchange and the interactions among the amphiphilic products.

Representative motifs include reversible Diels–Alder adducts, Schiff bases, disulfides, and boronate esters [54–59]. In self-healing hydrogels, exchangeable bonds reconnect damaged network segments, while hydrogen bonding, electrostatic interactions, or coordination facilitate local rearrangement. These systems show how dynamic covalent exchange supports adaptive network behavior. However, recovery after damage does not alone establish that the initial network was formed by self-assembly.

Lin et al. transferred this adaptive-network concept to a crosslinked binder for silicon-based anodes [60]. Tannic acid (TA), carboxymethyl cellulose (CMC), and boric acid (BA) formed a network containing multiple hydrogen bonds and dynamic boronate ester crosslinks. During repeated silicon expansion and contraction, hydrogen bonds dissociated preferentially and dissipated stress, while boronate ester exchange enabled network rearrangement and reconnection [60]. These coupled processes helped maintain contact among active particles under the reported cycling conditions. The electrochemical response, however, also depends on binder distribution, electrode architecture, and testing conditions.

### 中文对照

动态共价化学将可交换的共价键引入组装体和聚合物网络 [53]。这类键可以在一组条件下保持共价连接，并在另一组条件下发生交换，使网络无需完全溶解即可重排。在自组装过程中，这种可逆性有助于纠错和结构重组，而组装产物之间的结合方式仍由非共价作用共同决定。Wang 等利用动态共价成键构筑了偶氮苯–半乳糖吡喃糖苷超两亲分子，并获得了具有双重响应的组装行为 [52]。因此，该组装结构同时来源于共价交换以及两亲性产物之间的相互作用。

代表性的动态共价基元包括可逆 Diels–Alder 加合物、Schiff 碱、二硫键和硼酸酯 [54–59]。在自修复水凝胶中，可交换共价键能够重新连接受损的网络链段，氢键、静电作用或配位作用则有助于局部结构重排。这些体系说明动态共价交换可以赋予网络自适应行为。不过，损伤后的恢复本身并不能证明初始网络由自组装形成。

Lin 等将这种自适应网络思路用于硅基负极的交联黏结剂 [60]。单宁酸（TA）、羧甲基纤维素（CMC）和硼酸（BA）形成了同时含有多重氢键和动态硼酸酯交联的网络。在硅反复膨胀和收缩时，氢键优先解离并耗散应力，硼酸酯交换则使网络能够重排和重新连接 [60]。这些协同过程有助于在所报道的循环条件下维持活性颗粒之间的接触。不过，最终电化学响应还受到黏结剂分布、电极结构和测试条件的共同影响。

## 3.2 External Field-Induced Self-Assembly

External optical, electric, and magnetic fields couple, respectively, to photoresponsive states, charge or dipole distributions, and magnetic moments, thereby altering assembly pathways [61,62]. They can change orientation, interparticle attraction, and activation barriers on temporal and spatial scales set by the applied field. Field-induced structures need not correspond to the global free-energy minimum. Depending on relaxation kinetics, a structure may persist after field removal, become kinetically trapped, or disassemble when the input ceases.

### 中文对照

外加光场、电场和磁场分别与光响应状态、电荷或偶极分布以及磁矩发生耦合，从而改变组装路径 [61,62]。外场能够在其作用时间和空间范围内调节构筑单元取向、颗粒间吸引以及组装能垒。外场诱导形成的结构并不必然对应全局自由能最低态。根据弛豫动力学的不同，结构可能在撤场后继续保留、陷入动力学受限状态，或在能量输入停止后发生解组装。

## 3.2.1 Optical Field Induction

Optical fields regulate assembly through photochemical or photophysical processes that change molecular conformation, polarity, or intermolecular affinity [63]. Azobenzene is widely used because light-driven trans–cis isomerization changes molecular geometry and perturbs the interactions that stabilize supramolecular structures. Zhu et al. incorporated disperse red 1 (DR1) and 4-aminoazobenzene (Aazo) into poly(acrylic acid) (PAA), then combined PAA with poly(ethylene oxide) (PEO) to form hydrogen-bonded fibers [64]. Light-induced isomerization reorganized the fibrous network and altered its initial modulus and yield stress [64]. This study connects a defined molecular switch to a mesoscale structural and mechanical response. The extent of reorganization nevertheless depends on hydrogen-bond reversibility and polymer mobility, which may become limiting as liquid content decreases or mechanical confinement increases.

### 中文对照

光场通过光化学或光物理过程改变分子构象、极性或分子间亲和力，从而调控组装行为 [63]。偶氮苯被广泛用于此类体系，因为光诱导的 trans–cis 异构化会改变分子几何结构，并扰动维持超分子结构的相互作用。Zhu 等将分散红 1（DR1）和 4-氨基偶氮苯（Aazo）引入聚丙烯酸（PAA），随后将 PAA 与聚氧化乙烯（PEO）复合，形成氢键纤维 [64]。光诱导异构化使纤维网络发生重组，并改变了材料的初始模量和屈服应力 [64]。该研究将明确的分子开关与介观尺度的结构及力学响应联系起来。不过，重组程度仍取决于氢键可逆性和聚合物迁移能力；随着液体含量降低或机械约束增强，这两个条件可能成为限制因素。
