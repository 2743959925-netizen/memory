## 英文修改稿

A related example uses a functional additive to direct local self-assembly in a liquid sodium-ion battery electrolyte. Yao et al. [99] selected 15-crown-5 (15-C-5) through theoretical screening of ether molecules. Its strong ion–dipole interaction with Na⁺ promotes host–guest association and the in situ formation of the supramolecular complex [Na(15-C-5)]⁺. The high rigidity and bulky size of 15-C-5 limit its local population around Na⁺ and leave coordination sites for PF₆⁻. PF₆⁻ can therefore participate in the primary solvation shell and favor the formation of contact ion pairs (CIPs). This anion-engaged structure reduces the contribution of free diethylene glycol dimethyl ether (DGM) molecules to Na⁺ coordination and changes the local solvation environment. The reorganization was associated with faster Na⁺ transport at low temperature and a lower electrolyte freezing point. It also suppressed reported electrolyte side reactions and favored a thin, NaF-rich solid–electrolyte interphase (SEI) with lower interfacial resistance. These changes may facilitate interfacial Na⁺ transfer through the combined effects of solvation reorganization and SEI evolution. Under the reported compositions, the additive improved the electrochemical performance of several sodium-ion anodes across a wider temperature range.

## 中文对照

另一项相关研究利用功能添加剂引导液态钠离子电池电解液中的局部自组装。Yao 等[99]通过对醚类分子进行理论筛选，选定 15-冠-5（15-C-5）作为添加剂。其与 Na⁺ 之间较强的离子–偶极相互作用促进了主–客体结合，并诱导超分子复合物 [Na(15-C-5)]⁺ 原位形成。15-C-5 具有较高的刚性和较大的分子体积，因此其在 Na⁺ 周围的局部数量受到限制，并为 PF₆⁻ 留出配位位点。由此，PF₆⁻ 可以参与第一溶剂化壳层，并有利于接触离子对（CIPs）的形成。该阴离子参与的结构减少了游离二乙二醇二甲醚（DGM）分子对 Na⁺ 配位的贡献，并改变了局部溶剂化环境。该结构重组与低温下更快的 Na⁺ 传输和更低的电解液凝固点相关。它还抑制了文献报道的电解液副反应，并有利于形成较薄、富 NaF 且界面阻抗较低的固体电解质界面（SEI）。这些变化可能通过溶剂化结构重组和 SEI 演化的共同作用，促进界面 Na⁺ 传输。在文献报道的组成条件下，该添加剂提高了多种钠离子电池负极在更宽温度范围内的电化学性能。
