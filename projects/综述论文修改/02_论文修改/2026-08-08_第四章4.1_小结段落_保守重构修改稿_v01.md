## 英文修改稿

Taken together, these studies indicate that electrode and current-collector morphology and three-dimensional (3D) architecture are closely linked to electrochemical kinetics and interfacial stability. In liquid-electrolyte systems, electrolyte wetting can support transport through porous electrode structures. In solid-state systems, transport and deposition depend more directly on continuous solid–solid contact and the connectivity of multidimensional pathways. The design focus therefore shifts from organizing components within a liquid-accessible network to reconstructing electrode and interface geometries under solid-state constraints.

综上，这些研究表明，电极和集流体的表面形貌及三维结构与电化学动力学和界面稳定性密切相关。在液态电解液体系中，电解液润湿能够支持多孔电极内部的传输。在固态体系中，传输和沉积则更直接地依赖连续的固–固接触以及多维传输通道的连通性。因此，设计重点也由在液态可进入网络中组织组分，转向在固态约束下重构电极和界面的几何结构。
