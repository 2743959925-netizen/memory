## 英文修改稿

Similar electrostatic charge matching has also been applied to anisotropic electrode networks in other secondary-battery chemistries. Huang et al. [92] assembled negatively charged SnNb₂O₆ with positively charged amino-functionalized reduced graphene oxide (En-RGO), forming a two-dimensional composite (Figure 9c). The conductive graphene matrix may provide physical confinement and accommodate deformation. The resulting sodium-ion battery anode delivered a reversible specific capacity of 300 mAh g⁻¹ at 50 mA g⁻¹. Luo et al. [93] alternately assembled positively charged exfoliated layered double hydroxides (LDHs) with negatively charged reduced graphene oxide (r-GO) nanosheets. Subsequent in situ sulfidation converted this precursor into a three-dimensional transition-metal sulfide (TMS) heteronetwork (Figure 9d). The assembled structure was reported to mitigate phase pulverization and volume expansion during prolonged sodium insertion and extraction. It retained a capacity of 589.9 mAh g⁻¹ after 1200 cycles at 0.5 A g⁻¹. Together, these studies suggest that electrostatic attraction between oppositely charged components can organize structures across multiple length scales and help build connected interfaces. Such multiscale organization may offer a structural route for integrating active and conductive components within liquid-electrolyte electrodes.

## 中文对照

类似的静电电荷匹配也被用于其他二次电池体系中的各向异性电极网络构筑。Huang 等[92]将带负电的 SnNb₂O₆ 与带正电的氨基功能化还原氧化石墨烯（En-RGO）进行组装，形成二维复合结构（Figure 9c）。高导电石墨烯基体可能提供物理限域并缓冲形变。所得钠离子电池负极在 50 mA g⁻¹ 下实现了 300 mAh g⁻¹ 的可逆比容量。Luo 等[93]则交替组装带正电的剥离层状双氢氧化物（LDHs）与带负电的还原氧化石墨烯（r-GO）纳米片。随后，通过原位硫化将该前驱体转化为三维过渡金属硫化物（TMS）异质网络（Figure 9d）。据报道，该组装结构有助于缓解长期钠嵌入和脱出过程中的相粉化与体积膨胀。在 0.5 A g⁻¹ 下循环 1200 次后，体系仍保持 589.9 mAh g⁻¹ 的容量。综合来看，这些研究表明，相反电荷组分之间的静电吸引可以组织跨越多个尺度的结构，并有助于构建连续界面。此类多尺度组织可能为液态电解液中活性组分与导电组分的整合提供结构路径。
