## D. 修订后的英文

Here, we connect the physicochemical basis of self-assembly with its use in rechargeable metal batteries. The discussion moves from the thermodynamics and kinetics of assembly to the ways in which molecular interactions and external fields organize electrodes, electrolytes, and interfaces. At the centre of the Review is a simple question: can strategies developed in liquid electrolytes still work when molecular motion is restricted and the interface becomes mechanically constrained? Comparing liquid and solid-state systems allows us to show where these ideas can be carried over, where they must be redesigned, and where the available evidence remains preliminary. In this way, the Review defines both the opportunities and the practical limits of self-assembly in solid-state batteries.

## E. 中文对照

本文将自组装的物理化学基础与其在可充电金属电池中的应用联系起来。讨论从自组装的热力学和动力学出发，进一步分析分子相互作用与外场如何组织电极、电解质和界面。贯穿全文的核心问题很直接：当分子运动受到限制、界面受到机械约束时，在液态电解液中形成的策略是否仍然有效？通过比较液态和固态体系，本文辨析哪些思路可以沿用，哪些必须重新设计，以及哪些方向目前仍只有初步证据。在此基础上，本文进一步说明自组装在固态电池中的应用机会和实际边界。
