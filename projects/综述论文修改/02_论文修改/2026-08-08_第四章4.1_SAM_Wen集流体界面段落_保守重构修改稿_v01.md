## 英文修改稿

Beyond three-dimensional reconstruction of active electrode frameworks, self-assembled monolayers (SAMs) provide another route for regulating metal nucleation and deposition at current collector/electrolyte interfaces. These layers use interfacial chemical bonding to modify the local surface chemistry of the collector. Wen et al. [94] used a thiol–Cu reaction to construct an organosilane layer on a planar Cu current collector (Figure 10a). The layer contained nanopores and a lithiophilic Si–O–Si surface. Under the reported ether-electrolyte conditions, this modification changed lithium deposition from a fine-grained, stacked-particle morphology to a more compact and laterally extended form. In full cells paired with a high-loading lithium iron phosphate (LiFePO₄, LFP) cathode, the resulting system operated for nearly 500 cycles. The modified collector also showed improved corrosion resistance, retaining a Coulombic efficiency of 97.12% after 300 min in a chloride-containing solution. This case illustrates how molecular surface modification can regulate lithium deposition morphology at a planar current collector.

## 中文对照

除对活性电极框架进行三维重构外，自组装单分子层（SAM）还可用于调控集流体/电解液界面处的金属成核和沉积。该类分子层通过界面化学键合改变集流体表面的局部化学环境。Wen 等[94]利用巯基–Cu 反应，在平面 Cu 集流体表面构筑了有机硅烷层（Figure 10a）。该层具有纳米孔，并形成亲锂的 Si–O–Si 表面。在文献报道的醚类电解液条件下，这种改性使锂沉积由细晶粒堆叠形貌转变为更致密、横向延伸的形貌。在与高负载磷酸铁锂（LiFePO₄，LFP）正极配对的全电池中，所得体系可运行近 500 圈。改性集流体还表现出更好的耐腐蚀性，在含氯溶液中腐蚀 300 min 后仍保持 97.12% 的库仑效率。该案例说明，分子层面的表面改性可以在平面集流体上调控锂沉积形貌。
