## 英文修改稿

Conventional liquid electrolytes such as ethylene carbonate/diethyl carbonate (EC/DEC) contain dynamically solvated ions distributed throughout a fluid solvent environment. Brownian motion promotes continual exchange between local solvation shells and the surrounding liquid. In the conventional localized high-concentration electrolyte (LHCE) picture, the diluent is selected to interact weakly with the target ion. It contributes little to the primary solvation shell. Concentrated salt–solvent clusters are therefore treated as dispersed within the diluent-rich matrix. This picture provides a baseline for discussing more localized electrolyte structures formed through self-assembly.
