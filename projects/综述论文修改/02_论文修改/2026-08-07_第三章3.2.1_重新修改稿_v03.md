## 3.2.1 Optical Field Induction

Light can direct self-assembly when photoexcitation changes molecular conformation and, in turn, the non-covalent interactions that stabilize an ordered structure [63]. Azobenzene-based systems provide a representative example because trans–cis isomerization changes molecular geometry and can reorganize surrounding supramolecular networks. Zhu et al. incorporated disperse red 1 (DR1) and 4-aminoazobenzene (Aazo) into poly(acrylic acid) (PAA). The modified PAA was then combined with poly(ethylene oxide) (PEO) to form hydrogen-bonded polymer fibers [64]. Upon irradiation, the azobenzene groups underwent trans–cis isomerization, which rearranged the fibrous architecture and increased both the initial modulus and yield stress. This result shows how a molecular photochemical event can propagate through a reversible hydrogen-bonding network to alter macroscopic mechanical properties.

Photoinduced electron transfer (PET) provides a related route for light-driven energy conversion and information transduction, although its structural response occurs at a different scale. PET converts optical excitation into directional electron transfer from an electron-rich donor to an electron-deficient acceptor. Its efficiency therefore depends strongly on the spatial relationship between the donor and acceptor. Huang et al. showed that conformational folding transformed a fluorophore–spacer–acceptor system from an open configuration into a folded one [65]. The reduced donor–acceptor distance enhanced the efficiency of the PET process. Unlike the fiber reorganization described above, this evidence concerns intramolecular conformational regulation rather than the formation of a larger self-assembled architecture.

## 中文对照

### 3.2.1 光场诱导

当光激发改变分子构象，并进一步调节维持有序结构的非共价作用时，光可以引导自组装过程[63]。偶氮苯体系是其中的代表，因为 trans–cis 异构化能够改变分子几何构型，并促使周围的超分子网络发生重组。Zhu 等将分散红 1（DR1）和 4-氨基偶氮苯（Aazo）引入聚丙烯酸（PAA）中，随后将改性 PAA 与聚氧化乙烯（PEO）复合，形成氢键聚合物纤维[64]。光照后，偶氮苯基团发生 trans–cis 异构化，进而引起纤维结构重排，并使材料的初始模量和屈服应力均得到提高。该结果说明，分子尺度的光化学事件可以通过可逆氢键网络向更高结构层级传递，最终改变材料的宏观力学性质。

光诱导电子转移（PET）为光驱动的能量转换和信息传递提供了另一种相关途径，但其结构响应发生在不同尺度。PET 将光激发转化为从富电子供体到缺电子受体的定向电子转移，因此其效率明显依赖供体与受体之间的空间关系。Huang 等发现，构象折叠使“荧光团–间隔基–受体”体系由开放构型转变为折叠构型[65]。供体与受体间距离的缩短提高了 PET 过程的效率。与上述纤维结构重排不同，这项证据所反映的是分子内构象调控，而不是更大尺度自组装结构的形成。
