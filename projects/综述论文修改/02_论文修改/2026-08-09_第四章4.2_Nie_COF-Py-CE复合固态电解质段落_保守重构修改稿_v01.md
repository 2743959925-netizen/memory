## 英文修改稿

Another solid-state example combines an ordered crown-ether-functionalized covalent organic framework (COF-Py-CE) with a fluorinated poly(vinylidene fluoride-co-hexafluoropropylene) (PVDF-HFP) matrix. The authors linked TFSI⁻ confinement to regions of high positive electrostatic potential and imine linkages in the COF. Polar C–F domains in PVDF-HFP provide an additional interaction environment. These features can limit anion motion through electrostatic adsorption and pore-defined confinement. Imine sites and crown-ether oxygens also provide exchangeable coordination sites for Li⁺ migration between adjacent COF layers. The calculated barrier for crown-ether-mediated interlayer hopping was 0.224 eV. This value describes a specific migration step, rather than the overall transport activation energy of the composite. At 30 °C, the electrolyte delivered an ionic conductivity of 1.15 × 10⁻³ S cm⁻¹ and a Li⁺ transference number of 0.91. Li||Li symmetric cells cycled for more than 1500 h at 0.5 mA cm⁻² and 0.5 mAh cm⁻². An NCM523 full cell retained 90% of its capacity after 2450 cycles at 1.0 C. A 1.2 Ah pouch cell retained approximately 95% of its capacity after 100 cycles. These results associate framework-based anion confinement and crown-ether-mediated Li⁺ hopping with the transport and cycling behavior of this composite electrolyte.

## 中文对照

另一个固态电解质案例将有序的冠醚功能化共价有机框架（COF-Py-CE）引入含氟聚偏二氟乙烯-六氟丙烯共聚物（PVDF-HFP）基体中。作者将 TFSI⁻ 的限域归因于 COF 中高正静电势区域和亚胺键的共同作用。PVDF-HFP 中极性的 C–F 结构还提供了额外的相互作用环境。这些结构可以通过静电吸附和孔道限域限制阴离子运动。COF 中的亚胺位点和冠醚氧还可作为可交换的 Li⁺ 配位位点，支持 Li⁺ 在相邻 COF 层之间迁移。计算得到的 0.224 eV 是冠醚介导层间跳迁这一特定步骤的能垒。它不应直接等同于复合电解质整体的离子传输活化能。在 30 °C 下，该电解质的离子电导率为 1.15 × 10⁻³ S cm⁻¹，Li⁺ 迁移数为 0.91。Li||Li 对称电池在 0.5 mA cm⁻² 和 0.5 mAh cm⁻² 条件下循环超过 1500 h。NCM523 全电池在 1.0 C 下循环 2450 圈后保持 90% 的容量。1.2 Ah 软包电池在循环 100 圈后保持约 95% 的容量。这些结果提示，在该复合电解质中，框架对阴离子的限域和冠醚介导的 Li⁺ 跳迁与其离子传输和循环行为相关。
