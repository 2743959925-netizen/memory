## 3.3.2 Crystallization- and Polymerization-Driven Self-Assembly

Unlike entropy-driven association, polymerization-induced self-assembly (PISA) and crystallization-driven self-assembly (CDSA) couple molecular organization to progressive changes in chain length, solubility, or crystallinity. These processes therefore define kinetic pathways that evolve together with the material itself.

In PISA, a solvophilic precursor block prepared by reversible-deactivation radical polymerization (RDRP) is chain-extended with a second monomer in a selective solvent [79]. The mixture remains homogeneous while the growing block is soluble. Once this block exceeds its solubility limit, the emerging amphiphilic block copolymers nucleate and assemble in situ. Continued polymerization changes the relative block lengths and drives transitions among spheres, worms or fibres, and vesicles. By integrating polymer synthesis with self-assembly, PISA can directly generate concentrated dispersions of block copolymer nano-objects, with solids contents reaching 40–50% under optimized conditions [79].

CDSA follows a different route because crystallization of a core-forming block directs growth along crystallographically permitted interfaces [80–82]. In living CDSA, preformed crystalline seeds separate nucleation from epitaxial growth, allowing particle dimensions and composition to be regulated through the unimer-to-seed ratio. Successful growth depends not only on lattice matching but also on crystallization temperature and the associated nucleation barrier [83]. This control has produced one-dimensional cylinders, nanowires, and hierarchical micelles [84–86], as well as two-dimensional platelets with patterned or segmented cores [87,88]. Thus, PISA couples morphology to polymerization-induced insolubility, whereas CDSA couples dimensional control to seed-mediated crystallization.

Poly(ferrocenylsilane) (PFS)-based block copolymers provide a mature platform for translating CDSA into functional hybrid structures. Lu et al. prepared water-soluble PFS-core micelles whose reductive cores supported in situ redox reactions [89]. Surface-initiated living CDSA then organized these micelles into brushes that templated vertical MnO₂ nanoarrays and Ag nanochain networks. This example connects controlled polymer crystallization with battery-relevant inorganic architectures, rather than treating CDSA solely as a route to polymer micelles.

For poly(ethylene oxide) (PEO)-based polymer electrolytes, analogous seed-directed architectures could separate a reinforcing crystalline scaffold from amorphous ion-transport regions. However, this concept remains to be demonstrated, and the crystalline fraction would need to be controlled without disrupting the continuous transport phase.

### 3.3.2 结晶与聚合驱动自组装

与熵驱动缔合不同，聚合诱导自组装（PISA）和结晶驱动自组装（CDSA）将分子组织过程与链长、溶解性或结晶度的持续变化相耦合。因此，这两类过程所规定的动力学路径会与材料本身同步演化。

在 PISA 中，首先通过可逆失活自由基聚合（RDRP）制备亲溶剂前驱体链段，再使其在选择性溶剂中引发第二种单体的链延伸[79]。当新生链段仍可溶时，体系保持均一。当该链段增长并超过其溶解极限后，新生的两亲性嵌段共聚物开始成核并原位组装。持续聚合改变了两种链段的相对长度，从而驱动形貌在球形、虫状或纤维状与囊泡之间转变。由于聚合物合成与自组装在同一过程中完成，PISA 可直接制备高浓度嵌段共聚物纳米分散体，优化条件下固含量可达 40–50%[79]。

CDSA 遵循不同的组装路径，其核心是利用成核链段的结晶沿晶体学允许的界面引导生长[80–82]。在活性 CDSA 中，预制晶体种子将成核与外延生长分离，从而通过单链与种子的比例调节颗粒尺寸和组成。成功生长不仅取决于晶格匹配，还受结晶温度及相关成核能垒的控制[83]。这种调控已实现一维圆柱、纳米线和层级微胶束[84–86]，以及具有图案化或分段核的二维片层[87,88]。因此，PISA 将形貌演化与聚合过程诱导的不溶化相联系，而 CDSA 则通过种子介导结晶实现尺度和维度调控。

聚二茂铁硅烷（PFS）嵌段共聚物是将 CDSA 拓展至功能杂化结构的成熟平台。Lu 等制备了水溶性 PFS 核微胶束，并利用其还原性核心支持原位氧化还原反应[89]。随后，表面引发的活性 CDSA 将这些微胶束组织成刷状结构，并进一步模板化生成垂直 MnO₂ 纳米阵列和 Ag 纳米链网络。该案例将可控聚合物结晶与电池相关的无机结构衔接起来，而不再将 CDSA 局限于聚合物微胶束的制备。

对于聚氧化乙烯（PEO）基聚合物电解质，类似的种子引导结构可能将起增强作用的晶态骨架与非晶离子传输区域分离开来。但该构想尚未得到直接验证，且必须控制晶相比例，避免破坏连续的离子传输相。

## 修改说明

- 将原稿的单一长段拆分为“PISA 路径—CDSA 路径—PFS 代表案例—PEO 设计启示”四个连续层次。
