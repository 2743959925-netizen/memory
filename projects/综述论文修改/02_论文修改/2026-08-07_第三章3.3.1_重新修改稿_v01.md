## 3.3 Thermodynamic and Kinetic Driving Forces

Unlike external fields, thermodynamic and kinetic routes encode the driving force within the evolving material system. Entropy gain, polymerization, and crystallization can therefore direct assembly by reshaping the free-energy landscape or the pathway through it.

## 3.3.1 Entropy-Driven Self-Assembly

Entropy-driven self-assembly occurs when structural organization lowers the free energy through an increase in system entropy (\(\Delta S>0\)), rather than through direct attractions alone. Two representative manifestations are depletion interactions in colloidal dispersions and hydrophobic association in aqueous media, with the following discussion focusing on depletion.

Within the Asakura–Oosawa framework, small non-adsorbing species are excluded from a shell surrounding each large particle [73,74]. When two large particles approach, these excluded volumes overlap and increase the free volume accessible to the smaller species. The resulting gain in translational entropy creates an osmotic-pressure imbalance that drives the large particles together [75,76]. Depletion attraction therefore arises from excluded-volume constraints, even when no specific attractive bond exists between the assembling particles.

At the nanoscale, the classical description depends on the relative dimensions and interactions of the components. Ofosu et al. examined oleate-capped indium oxide nanocrystals with polystyrene depletants [77]. Classical models reproduced the phase boundaries and colloidal structure when the polymer radius of gyration did not exceed approximately 80% of the nanocrystal radius. At larger size ratios, the measured attraction weakened, revealing the increasing influence of polymer physics. Onuh et al. further demonstrated depletion-mediated assembly of 1 µm polystyrene microcolloids on glass using 85 nm polystyrene nanoparticles, poly(acrylic acid) (PAA), or their mixtures [78]. Nanoparticles at 0.5% (w/v) produced well-ordered continuous coatings, whereas PAA produced irregular aggregates. Mixed depletants further increased aggregate size and substrate coverage relative to PAA alone. Together, these results identify depletant size, composition, concentration, and interfacial interactions as coupled controls over the final structure.

For battery processing, the same principle could provide a route to regulate particle packing and coating formation in multicomponent electrode slurries. Its applicability to solid electrolyte interphase (SEI) and cathode electrolyte interphase (CEI) formation remains to be established.

### 3.3 热力学与动力学驱动力

与外场诱导不同，热力学和动力学路径将驱动力编码于持续演化的材料体系内部。因此，熵增、聚合和结晶可通过重塑自由能景观或改变体系沿其演化的路径来引导自组装。

### 3.3.1 熵驱动自组装

熵驱动自组装是指结构有序化主要通过提高体系熵（\(\Delta S>0\)）来降低自由能，而非仅依赖构筑单元间的直接吸引。其代表性形式包括胶体分散体系中的耗尽相互作用和水相体系中的疏水缔合，下文重点讨论前者。

在 Asakura–Oosawa 框架中，小尺寸非吸附组分无法进入大颗粒周围的一层排除区域[73,74]。当两个大颗粒相互靠近时，它们的排除体积发生重叠，小组分可探索的自由体积随之增加。由此产生的平动熵增引起渗透压不平衡，并驱动大颗粒相互靠拢[75,76]。因此，即使组装颗粒之间不存在特异性吸引键，排除体积约束仍可产生有效的耗尽吸引。

在纳米尺度下，经典理论的适用性取决于各组分的相对尺寸和相互作用。Ofosu 等研究了聚苯乙烯耗尽剂作用下油酸封端氧化铟纳米晶的组装行为[77]。当聚合物回转半径不超过纳米晶半径的约 80% 时，经典模型可以再现相边界和胶体结构；在更大尺寸比下，实测耗尽吸引则明显减弱，表明聚合物物理行为变得更加重要。Onuh 等进一步以 85 nm 聚苯乙烯纳米颗粒、聚丙烯酸（PAA）或二者混合物作为耗尽剂，实现了 1 µm 聚苯乙烯微胶体在玻璃基底上的耗尽诱导组装[78]。0.5% (w/v) 的纳米颗粒可形成有序连续涂层，而 PAA 产生的是不规则聚集体；混合耗尽剂较单独使用 PAA 进一步增大了聚集体尺寸和基底覆盖率。这些结果共同表明，耗尽剂的尺寸、组成与浓度以及界面相互作用共同决定最终组装结构。

对于电池加工，同一原理可能为调控多组分电极浆料中的颗粒堆积与涂层形成提供新路径。但其能否用于调控固体电解质界面膜（SEI）和正极电解质界面膜（CEI）的形成，仍有待验证。
