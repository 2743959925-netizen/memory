## 3.2.2 Electric Field Induction

Electric fields direct colloidal assembly by polarizing particles and generating anisotropic dipole–dipole interactions. In a suspension, dielectric contrast between each particle and the surrounding medium creates an induced dipole under the applied field [69]. The resulting attraction promotes head-to-tail alignment along the field, producing one-dimensional (1D) chains [67,68]. When the dipolar interaction exceeds thermal energy (\(k_{\mathrm{B}}T\)), neighboring chains can associate laterally and develop higher-order structures. The accessible structures depend on particle concentration, interparticle potential and field strength. Reported transitions include isotropic fluids to string phases and body-centered tetragonal (BCT) crystals, as well as reversible face-centered cubic (FCC)-to-BCT transformations [66,68]. These outcomes reflect competition among dipolar, entropic and gravitational contributions, rather than relaxation toward a universal free-energy minimum [66].

Under alternating current (AC) fields, frequency provides an additional means of controlling these assembly pathways. In the kilohertz regime, interparticle interactions can include ionic polarization of the electric double layer (EDL) and dielectric polarization of the particle–medium system [68,69]. As the frequency approaches the megahertz regime, ion redistribution can no longer follow each field cycle. The EDL contribution consequently weakens, leaving dielectric polarization to dominate the induced dipolar interaction [69–72]. Field strength and frequency therefore regulate both the interaction magnitude and the structures reached during assembly, including disorder–order and crystal–crystal transitions [66,68,70,71].

### 3.2.2 电场诱导

电场通过极化颗粒并产生各向异性的偶极–偶极相互作用，引导胶体组装。在悬浮体系中，颗粒与周围介质之间的介电差异使颗粒在外加电场下产生诱导偶极矩[69]。由此产生的吸引作用促使颗粒沿电场方向首尾排列，形成一维（1D）链状结构[67,68]。当偶极相互作用超过热能（\(k_{\mathrm{B}}T\)）时，相邻链状结构可以进一步横向结合，形成更高层级的有序结构。最终可形成的结构取决于颗粒浓度、颗粒间势和电场强度。已有研究观察到从各向同性流体到链状相和体心四方（BCT）晶体的转变，以及可逆的面心立方（FCC）–BCT 晶体转变[66,68]。这些结果反映了偶极作用、熵效应和重力贡献之间的竞争，而不是体系必然趋向某一普适的全局自由能最低态[66]。

在交流（AC）电场下，频率为调控上述组装路径提供了另一维度。在千赫兹范围内，颗粒间相互作用可以同时包含双电层（EDL）的离子极化和颗粒–介质体系的介电极化[68,69]。当频率升高至兆赫兹范围时，离子重新分布无法跟随每个电场周期。EDL 的贡献因而减弱，诱导偶极作用逐渐由介电极化主导[69–72]。因此，电场强度和频率能够共同调节相互作用强度及体系可到达的组装结构，包括无序–有序转变和晶体–晶体转变[66,68,70,71]。
