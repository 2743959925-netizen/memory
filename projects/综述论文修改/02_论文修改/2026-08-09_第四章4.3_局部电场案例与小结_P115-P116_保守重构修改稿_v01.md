## 英文修改稿

### P115

Localized electric fields offer another way to redistribute charge carriers near metal interfaces. Dai et al. [112] used hexadecyl trimethylammonium chloride (CTAC) as a cationic surfactant additive in liquid lithium batteries. CTAC molecules accumulated near surface protrusions and formed a transient lithiophobic layer. The resulting electrostatic repulsion redirected incoming Li+ away from these protrusions. Li||Li cells cycled for about 300 h at 1.0 mA cm−2, and the electrolyte supported rates up to 4 mA cm−2.

This concept has also informed cathode and solid-polymer interface designs. Yang et al. [113] introduced boronation into a lithium-rich manganese oxide cathode, forming an ion-conductive surface and a BO3^3−/BO4^5− polyanion-gradient interface. The borated layer was associated with more reversible O/Mn redox chemistry and fewer side reactions at high voltage. The modified cathode delivered 305 mAh g−1 at 0.1 C and retained 92% of its capacity after 200 cycles at 1 C.

Dong et al. [114] covalently incorporated AMPS-derived anions into a polymer backbone to construct a single-ion sodium polymer electrolyte. The electrolyte showed a room-temperature conductivity of 4.3 × 10−4 S cm−1 and a Na+ transference number of 0.82. Its AMPSNa-rich surface formed a three-dimensional Na+ transport environment and promoted an F/O/N-rich interphase. In Na||Na3V2(PO4)3 cells, the electrolyte supported more than 1300 cycles at 4 C with 94.7% capacity retention.

### P116

Overall, self-assembly offers several routes for coupling interfacial chemistry with ion transport in liquid, quasi-solid, and solid-state batteries. The relevant design variables include contact geometry, local solvation or coordination, charge distribution, and the reversibility of interfacial interactions. Liquid systems can use wetting and mobile additives, whereas solid-state systems require persistent contact and mechanically compliant pathways. These differences limit direct transfer of a liquid interphase design to solid-state cells. Future progress will therefore depend on matching self-assembled structures to the phase state, operating conditions, and failure mode of each battery system.

## 中文对照

### P115

局部电场为重新分配金属界面附近的载流子提供了另一种方式。Dai 等 [112] 将十六烷基三甲基氯化铵（CTAC）作为阳离子表面活性剂添加剂用于液态锂电池。CTAC 分子在表面凸起附近富集，并形成暂时性的亲锂性较低的层。由此产生的静电排斥使进入界面的 Li+ 绕开这些凸起。Li||Li 电池在 1.0 mA cm−2 下循环约 300 h，所用电解液支持最高 4 mA cm−2 的电流密度。

这一概念也被用于正极和固态聚合物界面设计。Yang 等 [113] 将硼引入富锂锰氧化物正极，形成离子导电表面和 BO3^3−/BO4^5− 多阴离子梯度界面。该硼化层与更可逆的 O/Mn 氧化还原过程以及较少的高电压副反应相关。改性正极在 0.1 C 下提供了 305 mAh g−1 的比容量，并在 1 C 下循环 200 次后保持了 92% 的容量。

Dong 等 [114] 将 AMPS 衍生阴离子共价引入聚合物骨架，构建了单离子导电钠聚合物电解质。该电解质在室温下的电导率为 4.3 × 10−4 S cm−1，Na+ 迁移数为 0.82。富含 AMPSNa 的表面形成了三维 Na+ 传输环境，并促进了富含 F/O/N 的界面层形成。在 Na||Na3V2(PO4)3 电池中，该电解质在 4 C 下循环超过 1300 次后仍保持了 94.7% 的容量。

### P116

总体而言，自组装为液态、准固态和固态电池中界面化学与离子传输的耦合提供了多种路径。相关设计变量包括接触几何、局部溶剂化或配位环境、电荷分布以及界面相互作用的可逆性。液态体系可以利用润湿效应和可移动添加剂，而固态体系需要持续接触和具有力学顺应性的传输路径。这些差异限制了液态界面层设计向固态电池的直接移植。后续发展仍需要根据电池的相态、运行条件和失效模式匹配相应的自组装结构。

## 关键修改说明

1. 将原文“Hong [112]”纠正为“Dai et al. [112]”，因为当前参考文献 [112] 对应 Dai 等人的 CTAC 工作。
2. CTAC 案例保留阳离子表面活性剂、表面凸起富集、瞬态亲锂性较低层、静电排斥和 Li||Li 循环数据，但删除“通用策略”等扩展性判断。
3. Yang [113] 案例保留 BO3^3−/BO4^5−、富锂锰氧化物、容量和循环数据；将 O/Mn 反应调节写成“associated with”，避免把局部电场作为已被完全证明的单一因果来源。
4. Dong [114] 案例保留 AMPS-derived 固定阴离子、室温电导率、tNa+、F/O/N-rich 界面层和 Na||Na3V2(PO4)3 循环数据，并使用 AMPSNa-rich surface 与原文的材料命名保持一致。
5. P116 将总结句改为设计变量和适用边界的连续归纳，不再使用“完美验证”“关键理论桥梁”等超出综述证据尺度的判断。
