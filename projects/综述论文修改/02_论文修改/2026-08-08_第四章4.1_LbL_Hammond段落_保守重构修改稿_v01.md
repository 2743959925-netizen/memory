## 英文修改稿

In liquid systems, layer-by-layer (LbL) self-assembly can organize electrode components through electrostatic attraction or hydrogen bonding. This approach has been used to build porous frameworks with connected transport pathways and buffering capacity. In an early example, Hammond et al. [91] controlled polymerization kinetics in a protic acid medium. The process promoted electrostatic assembly between positively charged conjugated polyaniline (PANi) nanofibers and negatively charged functionalized multi-walled carbon nanotubes (MWNTs). Charge complementarity enabled these components to form a macroscopic, long-range interpenetrating network. The resulting porous thin-film cathode had a high specific surface area (Figure 9a). Its interconnected structure could facilitate lateral electrolyte wetting and help homogenize local ion flux.

## 中文对照

在液态体系中，逐层（LbL）自组装可通过静电吸引或氢键组织电极组分。该方法已被用于构筑具有连续传输通道和缓冲能力的多孔框架。早期研究中，Hammond 等[91]在质子酸介质中调控聚合动力学。该过程促进了带正电的共轭聚苯胺（PANi）纳米纤维与带负电的功能化多壁碳纳米管（MWNTs）之间的静电组装。电荷互补使这些组分形成宏观尺度的长程互穿网络。所得多孔薄膜正极具有较高比表面积（Figure 9a）。其互连结构可促进电解液横向润湿，并有助于均化局部离子通量。
