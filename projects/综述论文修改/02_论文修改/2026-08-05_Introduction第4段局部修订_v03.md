## D. 替换后的英文

Self-assembly offers a bottom-up strategy for constructing functional materials across multiple length scales. By translating interactions among molecular or colloidal building blocks into organized structures, self-assembly has been applied in fields ranging from biomedicine and photovoltaics to catalysis and energy storage. Representative examples include vesicular assemblies for molecular encapsulation [15], self-assembled monolayers for interfacial defect passivation in photovoltaic devices [16], and hierarchically organized polymer structures formed through crystallization-driven and supramolecular assembly [17] (Figure 2). Despite their differences in composition and function, these systems demonstrate the capacity of self-assembly to regulate spatial organization, interfacial chemistry, and dynamic structural evolution under comparatively mild processing conditions. These capabilities are particularly relevant to rechargeable batteries, in which ion transport, interfacial reactions, and structural stability are strongly influenced by the organization of electrodes, electrolytes, and their interfaces.

## E. 中文对照

自组装为跨越多个长度尺度构筑功能材料提供了一种自下而上的策略。通过将分子或胶体构筑单元之间的相互作用转化为有组织的结构，自组装已被应用于生物医学、光伏、催化和能源储存等多个领域。代表性例子包括用于分子包载的囊泡组装体 [15]、用于光伏器件界面缺陷钝化的自组装单分子层 [16]，以及通过结晶驱动和超分子组装形成的层级聚合物结构 [17]（Figure 2）。尽管这些体系在组成和功能上存在差异，它们共同体现了自组装在相对温和的加工条件下调控空间组织、界面化学和动态结构演化的能力。这些能力与可充电电池密切相关，因为离子传输、界面反应和结构稳定性均会受到电极、电解质及其界面组织方式的显著影响。

下一段首句调整为：

> 因此，在可充电金属电池中，自组装已被用于调控金属成核、离子通量、溶剂化环境和界面膜化学。
