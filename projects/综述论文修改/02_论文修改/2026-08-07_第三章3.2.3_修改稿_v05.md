## 3.2.3 Magnetic Field Induction

Magnetic fields direct self-assembly by coupling to the permanent or induced magnetic moments of the building blocks. The field aligns these moments, generating anisotropic dipole–dipole attractions along the field direction. When the dipolar interaction exceeds thermal energy (\(k_{\mathrm{B}}T\)), particles can assemble into one-dimensional chains or higher-order aggregates. For anisotropic colloids, magnetic alignment can also regulate particle orientation while other processes determine positional ordering. Ding et al. combined magnetic-field guidance with convective assembly to fabricate three-dimensional photonic crystals from ellipsoidal particles [61]. The resulting superlattices displayed both positional and orientational order, together with particle-size-dependent structural colour. Their measured reflection spectra were consistent with theoretical simulations. This example shows how magnetic alignment can complement convective transport to organize nonspherical particles into ordered three-dimensional architectures.

### 3.2.3 磁场诱导

磁场通过与构筑单元的永久或诱导磁矩耦合来引导自组装。外加磁场使这些磁矩取向，并沿磁场方向产生各向异性的偶极–偶极吸引。当偶极相互作用超过热能（\(k_{\mathrm{B}}T\)）时，颗粒可以进一步组装成一维链或更高阶聚集体。对于各向异性胶体，磁场取向还可以调节颗粒方向，而其他过程则决定其位置有序性。Ding 等将磁场引导与对流组装结合，以椭球颗粒构筑了三维光子晶体[61]。所得超晶格同时具有位置和取向有序性，并呈现与颗粒尺寸相关的结构色；实测反射光谱也与理论模拟结果一致。该案例说明，磁场取向可以与对流传质协同，将非球形颗粒组织成有序的三维结构。
