## 英文修改稿

These liquid-electrolyte examples provide a starting point for considering self-assembly at solid-state interfaces, but their design rules cannot be transferred directly. Liquid electrolytes can wet porous electrodes through capillary effects, whereas solid-state cells rely on continuous contact between solid phases and connected ion-transport pathways. Under high-current cycling, non-uniform alkali-metal stripping at the current collector/SSE interface can generate local pores and stress concentrations. These defects may promote metal penetration along grain boundaries in inorganic electrolytes or through microscopic defects in polymers. Translating self-assembly to the solid-state anode/current collector interface requires non-covalent interactions that can reorganize during cycling and interfacial molecules with tunable chemistry. The design objective is a compliant interphase that supports ion transport while accommodating interfacial deformation.

## 中文对照

这些液态电解液中的案例为考察固态界面中的自组装提供了起点，但其设计规则不能直接照搬。液态电解液可通过毛细作用润湿多孔电极，而固态电池中的离子传输依赖固相之间的连续接触和相互连通的传输通道。在高电流循环下，集流体/SSE 界面处不均匀的碱金属剥离可能产生局部孔洞和应力集中。这些缺陷可能促进金属沿无机电解质晶界或穿过聚合物微观缺陷发生穿透。将自组装策略迁移至固态负极/集流体界面，需要能够在循环过程中发生重排的非共价相互作用，以及化学性质可调的界面分子。这类设计旨在构建具有顺应性的界面层，使其在支持离子传输的同时适应界面变形。
