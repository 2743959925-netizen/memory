## 英文修改稿

Efaw et al. [97] examined a ternary LHCE containing lithium bis(fluorosulfonyl)imide (LiFSI) and 1,2-dimethoxyethane (DME). The diluent was tris(2,2,2-trifluoroethyl) orthoformate (TFEO). LiFSI dissolves in DME but has little solubility in TFEO, whereas DME and TFEO are miscible. This combination allows DME to act as a surfactant between salt-rich and diluent-rich regions. The electrolyte therefore forms a micelle-like network of salt–solvent clusters surrounded by a TFEO-rich matrix. The distribution of DME is governed by the balance between solubility and interfacial interactions, which stabilizes this localized structure. The network increases the local salt concentration and the fraction of AGG⁺ species, and these changes are associated with SEI evolution and lithium-metal cycling.

Cao et al. [98] also examined the LiFSI/DME–TFEO electrolyte system. TFEO contributes little to the primary Li⁺ solvation shell, while its electron-withdrawing groups can lower the electrolyte HOMO level and widen the electrochemical stability window. Interfacial evolution of this solvation structure produced a monolithic SEI with a LiF-rich inner phase. The resulting interphase was associated with more uniform lithium deposition and reduced fluctuations in interfacial resistance. Under the reported conditions, it also mitigated lithium pulverization and dead-lithium formation. Li||NCM811 cells using this electrolyte supported charge–discharge at 4 C.

## 中文对照

Efaw 等[97]以双（氟磺酰）亚胺锂（LiFSI）、1,2-二甲氧基乙烷（DME）和三（2,2,2-三氟乙基）原甲酸酯（TFEO）构建三元 LHCE，并考察其微观结构。LiFSI 可溶于 DME，但在 TFEO 中的溶解度很低；DME 与 TFEO 则具有较高互溶性。该组合使 DME 能够在富盐区域和富稀释剂区域之间发挥类似表面活性剂的作用。由此形成了由盐–溶剂簇构成、被富 TFEO 基体包围的胶束状网络。DME 的分布由溶解度和界面相互作用之间的平衡决定，从而稳定了这种局域化结构。该网络提高了局部盐浓度和 AGG⁺ 物种的比例，而这些变化与 SEI 演化及锂金属电池循环行为相关。

Cao 等[98]进一步考察了 LiFSI/DME–TFEO 电解液体系。TFEO 对 Li⁺ 第一溶剂化壳层的贡献较小，其吸电子基团则可能降低电解液的 HOMO 能级并拓宽电化学稳定窗口。该溶剂化结构在界面处演化，形成具有富 LiF 内层的整体式 SEI。所得界面层与更均匀的锂沉积和较小的界面阻抗波动相关。在文献报道条件下，该界面层还减缓了锂粉化和死锂形成。采用该电解液的 Li||NCM811 电池可在 4 C 下进行充放电测试。
