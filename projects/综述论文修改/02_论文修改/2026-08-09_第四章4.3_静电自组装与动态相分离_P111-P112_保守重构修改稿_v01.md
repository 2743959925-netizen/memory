# 第四章 4.3 静电自组装与动态相分离：P112–P113 保守重构修改稿

## 英文修改稿

### P111

Electrostatic interactions provide a second route for organizing interfacial charge and ion transport. Their design principles are not directly transferable from liquid to solid-state cells. Liquid electrolytes can wet porous electrodes, whereas solid-state cells require continuous contact between solid phases and connected ion-transport pathways. During high-current cycling, non-uniform metal stripping can generate pores and stress concentrations at the current collector/SSE interface. These defects may promote penetration along inorganic grain boundaries or through microscopic defects in polymers. Solid-state designs therefore require interfacial molecules that combine tunable chemistry with interactions that can reorganize during cycling. A compliant interphase must support ion transport while accommodating local deformation. Earlier liquid-cell studies used Cs+ additives and ordered organic/inorganic interphases to redirect Li deposition and stabilize the metal interface [108,109].

### P112

At solid-state electrolyte/current-collector interfaces, molecular self-assembly is being explored to regulate lithium nucleation and interfacial chemistry. Yin et al. [95] anchored 5-nitro-2-mercaptobenzimidazole (N-MBI) on a Cu current collector through Cu–S bonding. Before lithium deposition, N-MBI formed an ultrathin non-sacrificial interphase and converted in situ to Li-NH2-MBI during initial lithiation. This transformation increased lithium affinity at the interface and was associated with more uniform nucleation beneath the layer. Li|Cu cells reached an average Coulombic efficiency of 99.3% under the reported conditions.

Zhang et al. [110] used 3-mercaptopropyl trimethoxysilane (MPTS) to form a Zn–S-linked layer on Zn. They then introduced trimethoxy(3,3,3-trifluoropropyl) silane (TTFPS) through hydrolysis and condensation. The resulting polysilane interphase combined siloxane and CF3 groups. Zn cells reported a Coulombic efficiency of about 99.8% after 1500 cycles at 1 mA cm−2.

Related self-assembled interphases have also been studied in liquid-electrolyte lithium-metal cells. Gao et al. [96] formed an electrochemically active monolayer on Cu using 1,3-benzenedisulfonyl fluoride. The layer altered the interfacial chemistry and contributed to a LiF-rich multilayer SEI. It was associated with more homogeneous lithium deposition over a wide temperature range. Together, these examples connect molecular surface chemistry with nucleation and interphase evolution across different electrolyte environments.

A related approach uses electrostatic interactions to induce phase separation within a solid polymer electrolyte. Zhang et al. [111] used LiTFSI-mediated in situ polymerization to form a poly(vinylene carbonate) (PVC)-based dual-phase electrolyte. Interactions between TFSI− and the polymer segments drove self-organized phase separation. The resulting architecture combined a mechanically supportive phase with connected pathways for Li+ transport. At 25 °C, the electrolyte showed a conductivity of 0.60 mS cm−1 and a Li+ transference number of 0.78. Li|PVC|LFP cells retained 90% of their capacity after 4000 cycles at 5 C. These results associate salt-regulated phase separation with ion transport and interfacial stability in this polymer electrolyte.

## 中文对照

### P111

静电作用为组织界面电荷和离子传输提供了另一条路径。其设计原则不能直接从液态电池移植到固态电池。液态电解液可以润湿多孔电极，而固态电池依赖连续的固–固接触和连通的离子传输路径。在高电流循环过程中，集流体/SSE 界面处不均匀的金属剥离可能产生孔隙和应力集中。这些缺陷可能促进金属沿无机电解质晶界或聚合物中的微观缺陷继续穿透。因此，固态体系需要同时具备可调化学性质和循环过程中可重组相互作用的界面分子。柔性的界面层需要在支持离子传输的同时容纳局部变形。较早的液态体系研究利用 Cs+ 添加剂和有序有机/无机界面层调节锂沉积并稳定金属界面 [108,109]。

### P112

在固态电解质/集流体界面处，分子自组装已被用于调节锂成核和界面化学。Yin 等 [95] 通过 Cu–S 键将 5-硝基-2-巯基苯并咪唑（N-MBI）锚定在 Cu 集流体上。在锂沉积前，N-MBI 形成超薄的非牺牲界面层，并在初始嵌锂过程中原位转化为 Li-NH2-MBI。该转化提高了界面对锂的亲和性，并与界面层下方更均匀的成核相关。Li|Cu 电池在所报道条件下实现了 99.3% 的平均库仑效率。

Zhang 等 [110] 使用 3-巯丙基三甲氧基硅烷（MPTS）在 Zn 表面形成 Zn–S 连接层，随后通过水解和缩合引入三甲氧基（3,3,3-三氟丙基）硅烷（TTFPS）。所得聚硅烷界面层同时包含硅氧烷和 CF3 基团。Zn 电池在 1 mA cm−2 下循环 1500 次后，报道的库仑效率约为 99.8%。

相关的自组装界面层也被用于液态电解质锂金属电池。Gao 等 [96] 采用 1,3-苯二磺酰氟在 Cu 表面形成电化学活性单层。该层改变了界面化学，并参与形成富 LiF 的多层 SEI。该界面层与较宽温度范围内更均匀的锂沉积相关。上述案例共同说明，分子表面化学可以在不同电解质环境中联系锂成核和界面层演化。

另一类方法利用静电作用诱导固态聚合物电解质发生相分离。Zhang 等 [111] 通过 LiTFSI 介导的原位聚合构建了基于聚碳酸乙烯酯（PVC）的双相电解质。TFSI− 与聚合物链段之间的相互作用驱动了自组织相分离。所得结构同时包含提供力学支撑的相和连通的 Li+ 传输路径。在 25 °C 下，该电解质的电导率为 0.60 mS cm−1，Li+ 迁移数为 0.78。Li|PVC|LFP 电池在 5 C 下循环 4000 次后保持了 90% 的容量。这些结果表明，在该聚合物电解质中，盐调节的相分离与离子传输和界面稳定性相关。

## 关键修改说明

1. 将 P111 的重点从“静电屏蔽具有普适性”改为“液态和固态体系的接触条件不同，因此需要重新设计界面相互作用”。
2. 保留 [108]、[109] 的液态电池背景，但仅将其作为固态界面设计的出发点，不把液态结果直接等同于固态结果。
3. 保留 [95]、[96]、[110] 和 [111] 的材料、结构转化及性能数据，分别对应 N-MBI、EAM、聚硅烷界面层和 PVC 双相电解质。
4. 将 [111] 的相分离结构明确写成“力学支撑相 + Li+ 连通路径”，使其与 4.2 节聚合物电解质中的结构–传输逻辑衔接，但没有添加原文没有给出的机理细节。

## 科学性核查

- Yin [95] 的作者、N-MBI、Cu–S 锚定、Li-NH2-MBI 原位转化和 99.3% 平均库仑效率与当前文献记录一致；该文同时报告了均匀成核/生长，正文使用“associated with”收紧因果强度。
- Gao [96] 的文献题名和 DOI 已通过 Nature Energy 页面核对：其核心是 Cu 表面的 EAM、LiF-rich SEI 和宽温度范围内的锂沉积调控。原稿把它归入液态电解质对照案例，保留这一位置。
- Zhang [110] 的 MPTS/TTFPS 组合、Zn–S 连接、缩合形成的聚硅烷层和约 99.8% 数据沿用原稿；“抗枝晶”没有作为普适结论写入。
- Zhang [111] 的 PVC 双相电解质保留了 0.60 mS cm−1、tLi+ = 0.78 和 5 C 下 4000 次后 90% 容量保持率；这些数据对应该电解质体系，不能推广至所有相分离聚合物。
