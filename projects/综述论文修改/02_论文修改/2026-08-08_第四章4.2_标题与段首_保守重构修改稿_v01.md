## 英文修改稿

### 4.2 Electrolyte Architectures: From Local Solvation to Long-Range Ion Transport

Liquid and solid electrolytes rely on different structural features to support ion transport. In liquid systems, intermolecular interactions shape local solvation structures and transient ion-transport environments. Solid-state electrolytes generally require continuous pathways through the electrolyte matrix. When anions are relatively immobile, these pathways can favor target-ion transport and a higher transference number. Low migration barriers can also contribute to higher room-temperature ionic conductivity, although the effect depends on composition and defect structure. In both liquid and solid systems, micro- to mesoscale organization can influence bulk transport and interfacial electrochemical reactions. Self-assembly offers a bottom-up route for organizing electrolyte components through hydrogen bonding, electrostatic interactions, van der Waals forces, and coordination bonds. Under suitable thermodynamic conditions, these interactions may generate ordered structures and modify the barriers for ion migration.

### 4.2 电解质结构：从局部溶剂化到长程离子传输

液态和固态电解质依赖不同的结构特征实现离子传输。液态体系中，分子间相互作用塑造局部溶剂化结构和瞬态离子传输环境。固态电解质通常需要在电解质基体中建立连续的离子传输通道。当阴离子具有相对较低的迁移能力时，这类结构可促进目标离子传输并提高其迁移数。较低的迁移能垒也可能有助于提高室温离子电导率，但实际效果取决于组成和缺陷结构。在液态和固态体系中，微观至介观尺度的结构组织都可能影响体相传输和界面电化学反应。自组装可通过氢键、静电作用、范德华力和配位键等作用，为组织电解质组分提供自下而上的路径。在适当的热力学条件下，这些相互作用可能形成有序结构，并改变离子迁移的能垒。
