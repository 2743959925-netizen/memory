## 英文修改稿

### 4 Self-Assembly: Bridging the Transition from Liquid to Solid Batteries

Self-assembly has already been used in liquid battery electrolytes through molecular design and microscopic structural regulation. Non-covalent interactions can reshape bulk solvation structures and promote ion dissociation. These effects help improve ionic conductivity and widen operating-temperature ranges. The move to solid-state batteries (SSBs), however, changes the transport problem. Solid-state electrolytes (SSEs) mainly include inorganic solid electrolytes (ISEs), solid polymer electrolytes (SPEs), and composite solid electrolytes (CSEs). ISEs, including oxides, sulfides, and halides, are often pursued because they can combine high ionic conductivity with chemical stability. In these crystals, ion migration no longer relies on mobile solvation structures. It depends on Li-ion hopping through an immobile anionic framework and a lithium-ion sublattice. Vacancies and interstitial sites often govern this hopping process.

SPEs follow a more diverse set of conduction modes. PEO-based systems use dynamic coordination between alkali-metal ions and ether oxygens. Segmental motion in amorphous regions then supports relay-like ion hopping. PVDF-based systems provide coordination sites through electronegative fluorine atoms. Residual solvents can further assist the formation of transport networks. Gel polymer electrolytes (GPEs), such as PMMA and PVA systems, retain a stronger liquid-like contribution. Their flexibility and compatibility with liquid-processing routes make them practical candidates for SSB fabrication. Their ion transport is dominated by solvent-confined diffusion. Polymer relaxation and functional-group site hopping can also contribute.

These differences explain why liquid-electrolyte design rules cannot be transferred directly to solid systems. Interfacial instability and slow interfacial ion transport remain persistent barriers for SSBs. Yet the two systems still share a common electrochemical constraint. Charge carriers must move directionally within micro- and nanoscale potential fields. This commonality makes liquid-battery experience useful for designing solid-state architectures. But phase-dependent transport mechanisms impose clear kinetic limits on direct translation. Self-assembly is useful here because it can organize matter across molecular, interfacial, and mesoscale levels. The relevant question is not whether liquid strategies can be copied. It is how their organizing principles should be adapted to solid-state constraints.

At interfaces, ordered molecular frameworks or reversible interphases can help regulate ion flux. They can also moderate alkali-metal deposition and local stress accumulation. Dynamic covalent and non-covalent interactions add a further design handle. Under suitable conditions, bond exchange and reversible association may accommodate cycling-induced cracks or defects. Such effects could extend battery lifetime when the interphase remains chemically and mechanically compatible. Thus, the solid-state use of self-assembly should be framed as conditional translation. It requires matching interaction strength, ion-transport pathway, and mechanical response to each electrolyte class.

### 4 自组装：连接液态电池与固态电池的转变

自组装已经通过分子设计和微观结构调控用于液态电池电解液。非共价相互作用可以重塑体相溶剂化结构，并促进离子解离。这些作用有助于提高离子电导率，并拓宽工作温度范围。然而，转向固态电池后，离子传输问题发生了变化。固态电解质主要包括无机固态电解质、固态聚合物电解质和复合固态电解质。无机固态电解质包括氧化物、硫化物和卤化物，常因兼具较高离子电导率和化学稳定性而受到关注。在这些晶体中，离子迁移不再依赖可移动的溶剂化结构。它依赖锂离子在固定阴离子框架和锂离子亚晶格中的跳迁。空位和间隙位点通常调控这一跳迁过程。

固态聚合物电解质具有更多样的传导模式。PEO 基体系依赖碱金属离子与醚氧之间的动态配位。无定形区的链段运动进一步支持接力式离子跳迁。PVDF 基体系通过强电负性的氟原子提供配位位点。残余溶剂还可以辅助形成传输网络。以 PMMA 和 PVA 为代表的凝胶聚合物电解质保留了更强的类液态贡献。其柔性以及与液态加工路线的兼容性，使其成为固态电池制造中的实用候选体系。其离子传输主要由受限溶剂中的自由扩散主导。聚合物链段松弛和官能团位点跳迁也可能参与其中。

这些差异说明，液态电解液中的设计规则不能直接转移到固态体系。界面不稳定和缓慢的界面离子传输仍是固态电池的重要障碍。不过，两类体系仍共享一个基本电化学约束。载流子必须在微纳尺度电势场中发生定向迁移。这一共性使液态电池经验能够为固态结构设计提供参考。但相态决定的传输机制差异，也给直接迁移带来了明确的动力学限制。自组装在这里的价值，在于它可以在分子、界面和介观层级组织物质。关键问题并不是液态策略能否被照搬，而是其组织原则如何适配固态约束。

在界面处，有序分子框架或可逆界面层可以帮助调节离子通量。它们也可以缓和碱金属沉积和局部应力积累。动态共价和非共价相互作用进一步提供了结构设计手段。在合适条件下，键交换和可逆缔合可能适应循环过程中产生的裂纹或缺陷。当界面层同时保持化学和力学兼容时，这类作用有望延长电池寿命。因此，自组装在固态体系中的应用应被理解为有条件的迁移。它需要将相互作用强度、离子传输路径和力学响应与具体电解质类型相匹配。
