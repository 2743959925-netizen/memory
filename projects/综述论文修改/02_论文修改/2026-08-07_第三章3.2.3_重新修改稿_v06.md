## 3.2.3 Magnetic Field Induction

Magnetic fields can direct self-assembly by aligning the permanent or induced magnetic moments of responsive building blocks. This alignment generates anisotropic dipole–dipole attractions along the field direction. When the dipolar interaction exceeds thermal fluctuations (\(k_{\mathrm{B}}T\)), magnetic particles associate into one-dimensional chains and can subsequently develop into higher-order aggregates.

Magnetic fields can also regulate the organization of molecular assemblies. Gao et al. used atomic force microscopy to examine how DNA concentration, magnetic-field intensity and direction, and the mica surface collectively affect DNA self-assembly [new reference]. Without an applied field, increasing the DNA concentration from 3.5 to 7.5 ng µL⁻¹ transformed dispersed, branch-like structures into networks with uniform polygonal meshes. At 3.5 ng µL⁻¹, a 3.5 mT horizontal magnetic field extended the DNA strands along the field direction and enabled individual double-stranded DNA molecules to be resolved. At 7.5 ng µL⁻¹, varying the field intensity produced large-scale networks, aligned vine-like arrays, and other higher-order patterns. These results show that DNA assembly is governed by the combined effects of molecular concentration and field conditions, rather than by magnetic-field strength alone.

## 中文对照

### 3.2.3 磁场诱导

磁场可通过诱导响应性构筑单元中的永久或感应磁矩取向来引导自组装。这种取向会沿磁场方向产生各向异性的偶极–偶极吸引。当偶极相互作用超过热涨落（\(k_{\mathrm{B}}T\)）时，磁性颗粒会首先连接成一维链，并可进一步发展为更高阶的聚集结构。

磁场同样可以调控分子组装体的组织方式。Gao 等利用原子力显微镜考察了 DNA 浓度、磁场强度与方向以及云母表面对 DNA 自组装的共同影响[新增文献]。在无外加磁场时，将 DNA 浓度由 3.5 ng µL⁻¹提高至 7.5 ng µL⁻¹，可使分散的枝状结构转变为具有均匀多边形网孔的网络。当浓度为 3.5 ng µL⁻¹时，3.5 mT 水平磁场使 DNA 链沿磁场方向伸展，并使单个双链 DNA 分子得以被观察。当浓度为 7.5 ng µL⁻¹时，改变磁场强度则产生了大尺度网络、定向藤蔓状阵列以及其他高阶形貌。这些结果表明，DNA 的组装形貌取决于分子浓度与磁场条件的共同作用，而非由磁场强度单独决定。
