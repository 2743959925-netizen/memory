## 英文修改稿

### 4.1 Electrode Architectures: From Two-Dimensional Rigid Contact to Three-Dimensional Dynamic Buffering

In solid-state batteries, alkali-metal anodes can develop localized shear stress during repeated plating and stripping. Heterogeneous volume change further weakens physical contact at solid-solid interfaces. These coupled effects distort local electric fields and increase the risk of dendrite growth along interfacial defects. Conventional slurry processing often gives limited control over ion-intercalation phases and conductive networks. Self-assembly offers a different route for organizing electrode components. Through anisotropic non-covalent interactions, it can guide spatial orientation and structural arrangement at nanoscale resolution. This design logic is relevant to three-dimensional (3D) composite electrodes. It may help combine carrier transport, lower interfacial resistance, and stress-adaptive buffering within one architecture.

### 4.1 电极结构：从二维刚性接触到三维动态缓冲

在固态电池中，碱金属负极在反复沉积和剥离过程中可能产生局部剪切应力。非均匀体积变化还会削弱固–固界面的物理接触。这些耦合作用会扰乱局部电场，并增加枝晶沿界面缺陷生长的风险。传统浆料加工通常难以精确控制离子嵌入相和导电网络的空间关系。自组装为电极组分的组织提供了另一条路径。通过各向异性的非共价相互作用，它可以在纳米尺度上引导空间取向和结构排列。这一设计逻辑与三维复合电极密切相关。它可能有助于在同一结构中结合载流子传输、较低界面阻抗和应力适应性缓冲。
