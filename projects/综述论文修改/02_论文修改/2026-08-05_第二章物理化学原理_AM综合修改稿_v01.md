## E. 润色后的英文

### 2. Physicochemical Principles of Self-Assembly

Self-assembly is governed by both the free-energy landscape and the kinetic pathways available to its building blocks [23,24]. Thermodynamics identifies the states favoured under specified conditions, whereas kinetics determines which states can be reached within an experimental or processing time. The two cannot be separated completely because kinetic barriers remain important even when the final structure is an equilibrium state. Conversely, nonequilibrium structures may persist without continuous energy input or may require sustained driving. Following the framework of Sorrenti et al., these outcomes can be grouped into three broad regimes comprising four states: equilibrium, metastable, kinetically trapped, and dissipative nonequilibrium [24] (Figure 3).

**(1) Thermodynamic equilibrium state.** At equilibrium, the system occupies the global minimum of its free-energy landscape under a defined temperature, composition, and pressure. No continuous external input is required to maintain the average structure, although molecular exchange and defect correction may continue [24]. The equilibrium morphology can change with solvent composition, salt concentration, or temperature, but it should not depend on the preparation history. Liu et al. showed that transient clusters formed through anisotropic patchy-particle interactions can nucleate protein crystals where a dense protein liquid is thermodynamically unstable [25]. This result illustrates that a thermodynamically favoured product may still be reached through a structured kinetic pathway.

**(2) Non-dissipative nonequilibrium states.** Metastable and kinetically trapped assemblies occupy local minima and do not require continuous energy or matter input [24]. A metastable assembly crosses a barrier comparable to the thermal energy, (k_\mathrm{B}T), and relaxes toward a lower-free-energy state on an observable timescale. A kinetically trapped assembly faces a much larger barrier and persists beyond the experimental observation window unless an intervention changes the pathway. The distinction between these states is therefore timescale-dependent rather than absolute. Preparation history, including cooling rate, solvent exchange, concentration path, and order of addition, can determine which local minimum is reached. Coated nanocrystals provide a related example because shell chemistry alters colloidal stability, dispersion, and the structures accessible during assembly [26].

**(3) Dissipative nonequilibrium state.** Dissipative assemblies are maintained away from equilibrium by a continuous supply of energy or matter and, where relevant, the removal of reaction products [24]. If the input stops, the structure relaxes toward an equilibrium or non-dissipative nonequilibrium state. In a simple activation–deactivation description, the assembled population grows when activation is faster than deactivation. A nonequilibrium steady state can be maintained when the two rates balance, even though molecular exchange and energy consumption continue. Once the fuel is depleted and deactivation dominates, the assembly decays. Boekhoven et al. used an alkylating agent to activate a molecular gelator transiently, producing a fibrillar network that disappeared as the system returned to equilibrium [27]. In batteries, structures present only under applied current, electric field, or ongoing interfacial reaction should therefore be distinguished from assemblies that persist after the driving force is removed.

These regimes describe how an assembled state is selected and maintained. The next section considers the molecular interactions and external stimuli that shape the corresponding free-energy landscape and kinetic pathways.

## F. 中文对照

### 2. 自组装的物理化学原理

自组装同时受到自由能景观和构筑单元可经历的动力学路径控制 [23,24]。热力学决定在特定条件下哪些状态在能量上更有利，动力学则决定这些状态能否在实验或加工时间内到达。二者不能完全分开，因为即使最终形成的是平衡结构，动力学势垒仍会影响其形成。相反，非平衡结构既可能在没有持续能量输入的情况下长期存在，也可能需要持续驱动才能维持。按照 Sorrenti 等提出的框架，这些结果可以归入三个广义区间，包含四种状态：平衡态、亚稳态、动力学陷阱态和耗散非平衡态 [24]（Figure 3）。

**（1）热力学平衡态。** 在给定温度、组成和压力下，平衡体系处于自由能景观的全局最低点。维持其平均结构不需要持续的外部输入，但体系内部仍可发生分子交换和缺陷修复 [24]。平衡形貌可以随溶剂组成、盐浓度或温度改变，但原则上不应依赖样品的制备历史。Liu 等发现，由各向异性 patchy-particle 相互作用形成的瞬态团簇，可以在致密蛋白质液相热力学不稳定的条件下诱导蛋白质晶体成核 [25]。这一结果说明，即使最终产物在热力学上更稳定，其形成仍可能依赖特定的动力学路径。

**（2）非耗散非平衡态。** 亚稳组装体和动力学陷阱组装体均处于自由能景观的局部最低点，而且不需要持续输入能量或物质 [24]。当体系需要跨越的势垒与热能 (k_\mathrm{B}T) 相当时，亚稳组装体可以在可观察时间尺度内向自由能更低的状态弛豫。动力学陷阱组装体面对的势垒则大得多，除非外部干预改变组装路径，否则它会在实验观察时间内持续存在。因此，两者的区别取决于观察时间尺度，而不是绝对不变的分类。冷却速率、溶剂交换、浓度变化路径和组分加入顺序等制备历史，都可能决定体系最终进入哪个局部最低点。包覆纳米晶体提供了一个相关例子，因为壳层化学会改变胶体稳定性、分散行为和组装过程中能够到达的结构 [26]。

**（3）耗散非平衡态。** 耗散组装体依靠持续的能量或物质输入维持在远离平衡的状态，并可能需要排出反应产物 [24]。一旦输入停止，体系将向平衡态或非耗散非平衡态弛豫。在简单的活化–失活描述中，当活化速率高于失活速率时，组装体数量增加；当两种速率相等时，宏观组装量可以保持稳定，但分子交换和能量消耗仍在继续，因此形成的是非平衡稳态。当燃料耗尽且失活占主导时，组装体逐渐解体。Boekhoven 等使用烷基化试剂对分子凝胶因子进行瞬态活化，形成纤维网络；随着体系回到平衡状态，该网络随之消失 [27]。因此，在电池中，应区分仅在施加电流、电场或持续界面反应时存在的结构，以及撤去驱动力后仍能保持的组装结构。

这些状态说明了组装结构如何被选择和维持。下一节将进一步讨论塑造相应自由能景观和动力学路径的分子相互作用及外部刺激。
