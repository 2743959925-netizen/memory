## 英文修改稿

At solid-state electrolyte/current collector interfaces, molecular self-assembly is being explored to regulate lithium nucleation and interfacial chemistry. These layers can combine coordination or chemical bonding with lithiophilic surface sites. Yin et al. [95] anchored 5-nitro-2-mercaptobenzimidazole (N-MBI) on a Cu current collector through Cu–S bonding in an anode-free solid-state configuration. Before lithium deposition, N-MBI formed an ultrathin self-assembled layer that acted as a non-sacrificial interphase (SNI). During initial lithiation, the layer was converted in situ to Li-NH₂-MBI. This transformation increased the interfacial affinity for lithium and helped guide more uniform nucleation and growth beneath the protective layer. Li|Cu cells achieved an average Coulombic efficiency of 99.3% under the reported conditions. Related self-assembled interphases have also been studied in liquid-electrolyte lithium-metal cells. Gao et al. [96] formed an electrochemically active monolayer (EAM) on Cu using benzene-1,3-disulfonyl fluoride. Benzenesulfonate termini bound to the Cu substrate and influenced subsequent solid electrolyte interphase (SEI) formation. The resulting SEI contained a dense multilayer structure with a LiF-rich inner phase and was associated with more homogeneous lithium deposition. Together, these examples illustrate how molecular surface chemistry can couple interfacial bonding, SEI evolution, and lithium nucleation across different electrochemical environments.

## 中文对照

在固态电解质/集流体界面，分子自组装正被用于调控锂成核和界面化学。此类界面层可将配位或化学键合与亲锂表面位点结合起来。Yin 等[95]在无负极固态电池构型中，通过 Cu–S 键将 5-硝基-2-巯基苯并咪唑（N-MBI）锚定在 Cu 集流体表面。锂沉积前，N-MBI 形成超薄自组装层，并作为非牺牲界面（SNI）。初始锂化过程中，该层进一步原位转化为 Li-NH₂-MBI。该转化提高了界面对锂的亲和性，并有助于在保护层下引导更均匀的成核和生长。在文献报道条件下，Li|Cu 电池的平均库仑效率达到 99.3%。相关的自组装界面层也被用于液态电解液锂金属电池。Gao 等[96]利用 1,3-苯二磺酰氟在 Cu 表面构筑了电化学活性单分子层（EAM）。苯磺酸根端基与 Cu 基底结合，并影响后续固体电解质界面（SEI）的形成。所得 SEI 具有致密的多层结构和富 LiF 内层，并与更均匀的锂沉积相关。综合来看，这些案例说明，分子表面化学可以在不同电化学环境中耦合界面键合、SEI 演化和锂成核过程。
