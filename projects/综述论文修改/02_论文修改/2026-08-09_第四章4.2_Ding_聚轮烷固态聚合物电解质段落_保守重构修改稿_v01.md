## 英文修改稿

In solid polymer electrolytes, self-assembly can organize polymer backbones and cyclic hosts into interconnected environments for Li⁺ transport. Ding et al. [100] used intermolecular hydrogen bonding to thread cyclic 18-crown-6 (18C6) onto a linear poly(ethylene glycol) (PEG) chain. Both PEG termini were then capped with a bulky hexamethylene diisocyanate trimer (HDIt). This process produced an end-capped three-dimensional network with a polyrotaxane-type topology. The assembled structure contains hydrogen-bond-rich nanoscale environments that may support directional Li⁺ migration. Its steric confinement can also restrict the free motion of bis(trifluoromethanesulfonyl)imide (TFSI⁻), favoring Li⁺-selective transport. Under the reported measurement conditions, the Li⁺ transference number reached 0.41, compared with 0.25 for the blank control. In a Li||Li symmetric cell, the electrolyte supported lithium plating and stripping for more than 400 h at 0.1 mA cm⁻². In a lithium iron phosphate (LFP) full cell, the electrolyte retained 96.5% of its capacity after 250 cycles at 0.5 C. The reported electrochemical stability window extended to approximately 4.8 V versus Li/Li⁺. These results associate molecular threading and end-capping with changes in Li⁺ selectivity, transport, and voltage tolerance in this specific electrolyte.

## 中文对照

在固态聚合物电解质中，自组装还可以将聚合物骨架和环状主体组织为相互连通的 Li⁺ 传输环境。Ding 等[100]利用分子间氢键，将环状 18-冠-6（18C6）穿入线性聚乙二醇（PEG）链。随后，以体积较大的六亚甲基二异氰酸酯三聚体（HDIt）封端 PEG 两端。该过程形成了具有聚轮烷型拓扑结构的端封三维网络。所得组装结构包含富含氢键的纳米尺度环境，可能支持 Li⁺ 的定向迁移。其空间限域作用还可以限制双（三氟甲磺酰）亚胺（TFSI⁻）的自由运动，从而有利于 Li⁺ 选择性传输。在文献报道的测试条件下，Li⁺ 迁移数达到 0.41，而空白对照为 0.25。在 Li||Li 对称电池中，该电解质在 0.1 mA cm⁻² 下支持锂沉积/剥离超过 400 h。在磷酸铁锂（LFP）全电池中，该电解质在 0.5 C 下循环 250 圈后保持 96.5% 的容量。文献报道的电化学稳定窗口约达到 4.8 V（vs Li/Li⁺）。这些结果提示，在该特定电解质中，分子穿入和端封与 Li⁺ 选择性、离子传输及耐电压性能的变化相关。

## 科学性核查

- [100] 原始文献支持通过分子间氢键将 18C6 穿入 PEG，并使用 HDIt 封端，形成分子自组装醚基聚轮烷电解质；该结构由固态/液态 NMR 等表征确认。
