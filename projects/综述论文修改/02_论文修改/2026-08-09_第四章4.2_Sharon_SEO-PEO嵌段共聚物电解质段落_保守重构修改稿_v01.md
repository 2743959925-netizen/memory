## 英文修改稿

Sharon et al. [102] compared a polystyrene-block-poly(ethylene oxide) (SEO) block-copolymer electrolyte with a PEO homopolymer electrolyte, both containing LiTFSI. Under suitable compositions, the SEO–LiTFSI system formed ordered, periodic morphologies with alternating PEO–LiTFSI and polystyrene nanodomains. The PEO-rich domains provide the main ion-conducting regions, whereas the insulating polystyrene domains can provide mechanical support. Their study further showed that the interfaces between these domains affect ion solvation and transport. As the Li/EO ratio increased from 1/12 to 1/5, PEO–LiTFSI conductivity decreased nearly threefold. SEO–LiTFSI conductivity remained approximately constant over the same range. Spectroscopy indicated that additional Li⁺ ions occupied more PEO ether-oxygen sites in the homopolymer. In the block copolymer, the fractions of complexed and uncomplexed EO sites remained approximately constant over this concentration range. Molecular-dynamics simulations further showed that associated ions tended to segregate near the domain interfaces. Interfacial sequestration of excess salt may preserve a more favorable balance of Li⁺ solvation states within the PEO-rich domains. This organization can help maintain ion transport as the salt concentration increases, while the rigid polystyrene phase supplies mechanical support. The result links nanoscale phase separation to the coupled solvation, transport, and mechanical functions of the polymer electrolyte.

## 中文对照

Sharon 等[102]比较了聚苯乙烯-嵌段-聚环氧乙烷（SEO）嵌段共聚物电解质与 PEO 均聚物电解质的离子溶剂化和传输行为，两种体系均含有 LiTFSI。在适当组成下，SEO–LiTFSI 体系形成了由 PEO–LiTFSI 和聚苯乙烯纳米域交替排列的有序周期性形貌。富 PEO 纳米域构成主要的离子传导区域，而绝缘的聚苯乙烯纳米域可以提供机械支撑。该研究还表明，这些纳米域之间的界面会影响离子溶剂化和传输。当 Li/EO 比值从 1/12 增加至 1/5 时，PEO–LiTFSI 的电导率下降近三倍，而 SEO–LiTFSI 的电导率基本保持不变。光谱结果表明，在均聚物电解质中，增加的 Li⁺ 会占据更多 PEO 醚氧位点。在嵌段共聚物中，配位和未配位 EO 位点的比例在这一浓度范围内基本保持不变。分子动力学模拟进一步显示，离子聚集体倾向于在纳米域界面附近发生分离。界面对过量盐的限域可能使富 PEO 纳米域内部保持更有利的 Li⁺ 溶剂化状态平衡。随着盐浓度增加，这种组织方式可能有助于维持离子传输，同时由刚性聚苯乙烯相提供机械支撑。该案例将纳米尺度的微相分离与聚合物电解质中的溶剂化、传输和力学功能联系起来。
