# 第四章 4.2 Electrolyte Architectures: From Local Solvation to Long-Range Ion Transport

## 英文整节修改稿

Liquid and solid electrolytes organize ion transport through different structural features. Liquid systems rely on local solvation and dynamic ion association. Solid systems require connected pathways within a mechanically constrained matrix. Restricting anion motion may increase the target-ion transference number, whereas lower transport barriers may support higher room-temperature conductivity. Across both phases, transport and interfacial reactions depend on organization from the molecular to mesoscale. Self-assembly provides a bottom-up route through hydrogen bonding, electrostatic interactions, van der Waals forces, and coordination bonds.

Conventional ethylene carbonate/diethyl carbonate (EC/DEC) electrolytes rely on exchange between local solvation shells and the surrounding liquid. In the conventional localized high-concentration electrolyte (LHCE) model, diluents contribute little to the primary solvation shell. Salt–solvent clusters therefore remain dispersed in a diluent-rich matrix. This model provides a baseline for more localized structures formed by self-assembly.

Efaw et al. [97] used lithium bis(fluorosulfonyl)imide (LiFSI), 1,2-dimethoxyethane (DME), and tris(2,2,2-trifluoroethyl) orthoformate (TFEO) to form micelle-like salt–solvent clusters in a TFEO-rich matrix. Limited LiFSI solubility in TFEO and miscibility between DME and TFEO organize DME at salt-rich/diluent-rich interfaces. This structure increases the local salt concentration and the population of aggregate (AGG⁺) species, and was associated with preferential reduction and improved lithium-metal cycling. In a related LiFSI/DME–TFEO system, Cao et al. [98] linked TFEO’s CF₃ groups with a lower highest occupied molecular orbital (HOMO) level. This process produced a LiF-rich monolithic solid-electrolyte interphase (SEI) at the interface. The interphase was associated with more uniform lithium deposition and less lithium pulverization and dead-lithium formation in Li||NCM811 cells cycled at 4 C.

Yao et al. [99] selected 15-crown-5 (15-C-5) through theoretical screening and used it as an additive for sodium-ion batteries. The additive formed [Na(15-C-5)]⁺ through host–guest association and reorganized the primary solvation shell. It favored contact ion pairs (CIPs), reduced free diethylene glycol dimethyl ether (DGM) coordination, and was associated with faster low-temperature Na⁺ transport, a lower freezing point, and a NaF-rich SEI. The reported anode performance improved across a wider temperature range.

These liquid-electrolyte examples converge on one point: self-assembly changes local solvation and ion association. Solid electrolytes require the same principle to be extended across a constrained matrix. Polyrotaxane networks, covalent organic framework (COF) nanochannels, and block-copolymer domains illustrate three routes.

Ding et al. [100] threaded cyclic 18-crown-6 (18C6) onto a poly(ethylene glycol) (PEG) chain through hydrogen bonding and capped both termini with a hexamethylene diisocyanate trimer (HDIt). The resulting polyrotaxane-type network combined Li⁺-coordinating sites with steric confinement for bis(trifluoromethanesulfonyl)imide (TFSI⁻). Its Li⁺ transference number reached 0.41, compared with 0.25 for the control, while Li||Li cells cycled for more than 400 h at 0.1 mA cm⁻². Room-temperature ionic conductivity increased from 1.12 × 10⁻⁵ to 3.48 × 10⁻⁴ S cm⁻¹, and the stability window extended to approximately 4.6–4.8 V versus Li/Li⁺. LiFePO₄ and LiNi₀.₈Co₀.₁₅Al₀.₀₅O₂ cells also showed improved cycling in the reported tests.

Nie et al. [101] incorporated crown-ether-functionalized COF-Py-CE into a poly(vinylidene fluoride-co-hexafluoropropylene) (PVDF-HFP) matrix. Electrostatic adsorption and pore confinement immobilized TFSI⁻, while crown-ether oxygens provided exchangeable sites for Li⁺ migration between COF layers. The reported 0.224 eV value refers to the crown-ether-mediated interlayer hopping barrier. At 30 °C, the composite reached an ionic conductivity of 1.15 × 10⁻³ S cm⁻¹ and a Li⁺ transference number of 0.91. Li||Li cells ran for more than 1500 h at 0.5 mA cm⁻² and 0.5 mAh cm⁻². NCM523 retained 90% of its capacity after 2450 cycles at 1 C, and a 1.2 Ah pouch cell retained approximately 95.65% after 100 cycles.

Sharon et al. [102] compared a polystyrene-block-poly(ethylene oxide) (SEO) electrolyte containing LiTFSI with a PEO homopolymer electrolyte. SEO formed PEO-rich conducting domains and polystyrene domains that provided mechanical support. As the Li/EO ratio increased from 1/12 to 1/5, PEO–LiTFSI conductivity fell nearly threefold, whereas SEO–LiTFSI conductivity remained approximately constant. Spectroscopy and simulations linked this contrast to different salt distributions: additional Li⁺ occupied more PEO ether-oxygen sites in the homopolymer, whereas associated ions accumulated near interfaces in SEO. This partitioning may help preserve the solvation balance within PEO-rich domains as the salt concentration increases.

Across the liquid examples, self-assembly mainly reorganizes solvation shells and ion pairs. Across the solid examples, it creates confined or phase-separated pathways that couple ion motion with matrix mechanics. The resulting function depends on composition, morphology, interfaces, and temperature.

Yan et al. [103] developed selectively fluorinated aromatic lithium salts (SFALS) for PEO-based solid polymer electrolytes. Hydrogen bonding between SFALS anions and PEO contributed to a triad-like Li⁺ solvation structure. The system altered Li⁺ coordination and interfacial chemistry and showed stable cycling under the reported conditions.

Self-assembly therefore links electrolyte architecture with charge-carrier transport across molecular and mesoscale lengths. In liquid systems, it tunes local solvation and ion association. In solids and quasi-solids, it organizes longer-range pathways while coupling ion transport with matrix mechanics. These functions depend on the resulting composition and morphology, rather than on self-assembly alone. The next section examines how these principles extend from bulk electrolytes to solid-state electrolyte interfaces.

## 中文对照

液态和固态电解质通过不同的结构特征组织离子传输。液态体系依赖局部溶剂化和动态离子缔合。固态体系则需要在受力学约束的基体中建立连续传输路径。限制阴离子运动可能提高目标离子的迁移数，而较低的传输能垒有助于提升室温离子电导率。在两类体系中，传输和界面反应都受到分子到介观尺度结构组织的影响。自组装可以通过氢键、静电作用、范德华力和配位键，从底层组织电解质组分。

常规碳酸乙烯酯/碳酸二乙酯（EC/DEC）电解液依赖局部溶剂化壳层与周围液体之间的交换。在传统局部高浓度电解液（LHCE）模型中，稀释剂对第一溶剂化壳层的贡献较小。因此，盐–溶剂团簇通常分散在富稀释剂基体中。这一模型为理解自组装形成的更局域化结构提供了参照。

Efaw 等 [97] 以双（氟磺酰）亚胺锂（LiFSI）、1,2-二甲氧基乙烷（DME）和三（2,2,2-三氟乙基）原甲酸酯（TFEO）构建了富 TFEO 基体中的类胶束盐–溶剂团簇。LiFSI 在 TFEO 中的溶解度有限，而 DME 与 TFEO 互溶，使 DME 分布在富盐区域和富稀释剂区域之间。该结构提高了局部盐浓度和聚集体（AGG⁺）物种的比例，并与优先还原和锂金属循环稳定性相关。在相关的 LiFSI/DME–TFEO 体系中，Cao 等 [98] 将 TFEO 中的 CF₃ 基团与较低的电解液 HOMO 能级联系起来。该过程在界面处形成了富 LiF 的整体式固态电解质界面（SEI）。该界面与更均匀的锂沉积以及更少的锂粉化和死锂形成相关；采用该电解液的 Li||NCM811 电池可在 4 C 下循环。

Yao 等 [99] 通过理论筛选选定 15-冠-5（15-C-5），并将其用作钠离子电池添加剂。该添加剂通过主–客体缔合原位形成 [Na(15-C-5)]⁺，并重组第一溶剂化壳层。它有利于接触离子对（CIPs）的形成，减少游离二乙二醇二甲醚（DGM）对 Na⁺ 的配位，并与更快的低温 Na⁺ 传输、更低的凝固点和富 NaF 的 SEI 相关。文献报道的负极性能在更宽温度范围内得到改善。

上述液态电解液案例说明，自组装主要改变局部溶剂化和离子缔合。固态电解质则需要将这一原则延伸到受约束的基体内部。聚轮烷网络、共价有机框架（COF）纳米通道和嵌段共聚物纳米相代表了三类结构路径。

Ding 等 [100] 通过氢键将环状 18-冠-6（18C6）穿入聚乙二醇（PEG）链，并用六亚甲基二异氰酸酯三聚体（HDIt）封端两端。所得聚轮烷型网络结合了 Li⁺ 配位位点和对 TFSI⁻ 的空间限域。其 Li⁺ 迁移数达到 0.41，而对照为 0.25；Li||Li 电池在 0.1 mA cm⁻² 下循环超过 400 h。室温离子电导率由 1.12 × 10⁻⁵ 提高到 3.48 × 10⁻⁴ S cm⁻¹，电化学稳定窗口约为 4.6–4.8 V（vs Li/Li⁺）。采用 LiFePO₄ 和 LiNi₀.₈Co₀.₁₅Al₀.₀₅O₂ 正极的电池在文献测试中也表现出更稳定的循环行为。

Nie 等 [101] 将冠醚功能化 COF-Py-CE 引入聚偏二氟乙烯–六氟丙烯共聚物（PVDF-HFP）基体。静电吸附和孔道限域固定了 TFSI⁻，冠醚氧原子则为 Li⁺ 在 COF 层间迁移提供可交换位点。文献报道的 0.224 eV 对应冠醚介导的层间跳迁能垒。在 30 °C 下，该复合电解质的离子电导率为 1.15 × 10⁻³ S cm⁻¹，Li⁺ 迁移数为 0.91。Li||Li 电池在 0.5 mA cm⁻² 和 0.5 mAh cm⁻² 下循环超过 1500 h。NCM523 电池循环 2450 次后保持 90% 的容量，1.2 Ah 软包电池循环 100 次后保持约 95.65% 的容量。

Sharon 等 [102] 比较了含 LiTFSI 的聚苯乙烯–聚环氧乙烷（SEO）嵌段共聚物电解质与 PEO 均聚物电解质。SEO 形成了富 PEO 离子传导纳米相和提供力学支撑的聚苯乙烯纳米相。当 Li/EO 比从 1/12 增加到 1/5 时，PEO–LiTFSI 的电导率下降近三倍，而 SEO–LiTFSI 基本保持不变。光谱和模拟结果将这一差异与盐的分布方式联系起来：在均聚物中，新增 Li⁺ 占据更多 PEO 醚氧位点；在 SEO 中，缔合离子则倾向于聚集在纳米相界面附近。这种分配方式可能有助于在盐浓度增加时维持富 PEO 纳米相中的溶剂化平衡。

综合上述液态案例可以看出，自组装主要重组溶剂化壳层和离子对。固态案例则表明，自组装可以形成受限或相分离的传输路径，使离子运动与基体力学性质发生耦合。最终功能取决于组成、形貌、界面和温度。

Yan 等 [103] 开发了用于 PEO 基固态聚合物电解质的选择性氟化芳香锂盐（SFALS）。SFALS 阴离子与 PEO 之间的氢键作用参与形成“三联体”型 Li⁺ 溶剂化结构。该体系改变了 Li⁺ 配位和界面化学，并在所报道条件下表现出稳定循环。

因此，自组装将电解质结构与载流子传输联系起来，作用尺度覆盖分子到介观尺度。在液态体系中，它调节局部溶剂化和离子缔合。在固态和准固态体系中，它组织长程传输路径，并使离子传输与基体力学性质形成耦合。这些功能取决于最终形成的组成和形貌，而不是由自组装本身单独决定。下一节将进一步讨论如何把这些原则从电解质体相延伸到固态电解质界面。
