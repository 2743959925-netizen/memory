## 英文修改稿

### P106

Solid-state electrolyte interfaces differ from liquid-electrolyte interfaces in contact geometry, but both can develop unstable chemistry and non-uniform ion transfer. In solid-state batteries, limited solid–solid contact may reduce the active interfacial area. In liquid cells, interfacial reactions and evolving deposits may instead destabilize the junction during extended cycling. These differences make self-assembly a shared interfacial design question, while the relevant structures and failure modes remain system-dependent.

### P107

Self-assembly can organize non-covalent networks and dynamic covalent motifs at these interfaces. Hydrogen bonds, electrostatic interactions, and coordination sites may increase interfacial affinity and help maintain connected ion-transport pathways. Reversible interactions can also accommodate local deformation as electrode materials change volume. Localized binding sites may influence metal-ion deposition and interphase formation. Self-assembled monolayers and multilayers provide related routes for regulating electric-double-layer structures and interfacial reactions [22]. Functional additives can further direct the formation of artificial solid electrolyte interphase (SEI) or cathode electrolyte interphase (CEI) layers. These approaches position self-assembly as an interfacial design tool whose effectiveness depends on contact geometry, chemical environment, and cycling conditions.

### P108

Among these interactions, hydrogen bonding provides a useful starting point because it combines reversibility with adaptable donor and acceptor sites. Hydrogen-bond networks can alter local coordination environments and interfacial charge distributions. Their effects can therefore extend from molecular association to interphase growth and mechanical response. Recent studies have applied such networks across liquid, quasi-solid, and solid-state battery components. The following cases show how hydrogen bonding is linked to interfacial chemistry, ion transport, and mechanical adaptation.

## 中文对照

### P106

固态电解质界面与液态电解质界面具有不同的接触几何，但两者都可能出现化学不稳定和离子传输不均匀等问题。在固态电池中，受限的固–固接触可能缩小有效界面面积。在液态电池中，界面反应和持续演化的沉积层则可能在长时间循环过程中使界面逐渐失稳。因此，自组装可以作为两类体系共同面对的界面设计问题，但其适用结构和失效模式仍取决于具体体系。

### P107

自组装能够在这些界面处组织非共价网络和动态共价结构。氢键、静电作用和配位位点可能提高界面亲和性，并帮助维持连续的离子传输路径。可逆相互作用还可以适应电极材料在体积变化过程中的局部变形。局域结合位点则可能影响金属离子沉积和界面层形成。自组装单层和多层膜也为调节双电层结构及界面反应提供了相应路径 [22]。具有特定官能团的添加剂还可以进一步诱导人工固态电解质界面膜（SEI）或正极电解质界面膜（CEI）的形成。由此，自组装可被视为一种界面设计工具，其作用取决于接触几何、化学环境和循环条件。

### P108

在这些相互作用中，氢键兼具可逆性以及可调节的供体和受体位点，因此适合作为界面调控的切入点。氢键网络可以改变局部配位环境和界面电荷分布。其影响也可能从分子缔合延伸至界面层生长和力学响应。近期研究已将此类网络应用于液态、准固态和固态电池组分中。下面的案例将说明氢键如何与界面化学、离子传输和力学适应性联系起来。
